>$$p=nk_{B}T$$
>$$dp=dnk_{B}T$$
$$n=n_{0}e^{-mgz/k_{B}T}$$
$$p=p_{0}e^{-mgz/k_{B}T}$$
$$dN=ndzdS$$
$$dN=n_{0}e^{-mgz/k_{B}T}dzdS$$
$$N=\int dN=\int^{\infty}_{0}n_{0}e^{-mgz/k_{B}T}dz$$
$$f(z)=\frac{dN(z)}{dz}. \frac{1}{N}=\frac{mg}{k_{B}T}e^{-mgz/k_{B}T}$$
